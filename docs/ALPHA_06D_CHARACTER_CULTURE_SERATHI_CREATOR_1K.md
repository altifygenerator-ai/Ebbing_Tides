# Alpha 0.6D — Production 1K Serathi Creator

Production 1K introduces the selected **Salt-Limestone Ledger** direction on the Character Creator only.

## Visual language

- Warm ivory paper and sun-bleached limestone dominate.
- Terracotta cloth and dark cedar structure the side dossiers.
- Dark cedar uprights and square hammered-copper joints replace Asterian-style metal rails.
- Terracotta tesserae and sparse indigo/sea-green glass squares provide a linear mosaic accent without becoming another wave band.
- No Covenant flame or invented secular emblem is repeated as Serathi culture ornament.
- No starburst, sun medallion, compass rose, circular boss, or continuous wave rail is used; those shapes are deliberately reserved away from the Serathi frame family.

## Runtime architecture

- The existing Creator DOM owns all layout, content, controls, and scrolling.
- One transparent nine-slice frame wraps the continuous central manuscript.
- The same frame is fitted independently to the portrait and existing art cells.
- A separate terracotta register skin wraps the six existing step buttons.
- Serathi regional paintings remain inside the existing lower rail and side-vignette wells.
- The responsive outer shell is intentionally unpainted; no full-screen frame is stretched to fit the viewport.

## Scope

The accepted Skeldran and Asterian Creator/Captain implementations are preserved. Serathi Captain remains gated until the Creator candidate is visually approved.
