# Arcane / Industrial Specialization — Alpha 0.6D Canon

## Product rule

There is **no normal player-facing Arcane ↔ Industrial allocation slider**.

The player does not spend points directly into a global Attunement value.

## Derived state

If the global compatibility state remains useful, it is derived from committed behavior such as:

- advanced Arcane practices learned and used,
- advanced technical techniques learned and used,
- meaningful accumulated Arcane / Industrial exposure,
- specialized schematics or equipment commitments,
- Arcana specializations and later equivalent technical specialization signals.

Ordinary characters should remain effectively neutral.

Using a pistol, sextant, ordinary pump, rope, common medicine, minor ward charm or other normal mixed-world tool does not by itself force a character into an extreme specialization.

## Qualitative player presentation

When there is a gameplay reason to surface the state, use qualitative language:

- Neutral
- Slightly Arcane
- Moderately Arcane
- Strongly Arcane
- Slightly Industrial
- Moderately Industrial
- Strongly Industrial

Do not prominently expose the exact internal number in normal play.

Neutral state should often require no special UI treatment at all.

## Compatibility rule

Meaningful incompatibility belongs primarily to **advanced specialization**.

High Arcane commitment may affect advanced precision machinery, experimental Industrial systems or other sensitive high-order technology.

High Industrial commitment may affect advanced wards, resonance work, ritual systems or sensitive Arcane equipment.

Low-level/common magic and common technology coexist.

## Gameplay proof requirement

The system earns long-term survival only if it creates real differences in play.

Arcane specialization must be capable of changing choices involving, for example:

- advanced wards,
- ritual navigation,
- resonance sensing,
- Arcane equipment,
- supernatural environmental solutions,
- certain knowledge/dialogue,
- ship warding,
- unusual exploration.

Industrial specialization must be capable of changing choices involving, for example:

- precision weapons,
- advanced engineering,
- pumps / damage control,
- navigation instruments,
- machinery,
- targeting,
- experimental propulsion,
- Industrial ship modules,
- technical alternate solutions.

If later prototyping cannot demonstrate meaningful differentiation without gimmicks, removing the global Attunement mechanic entirely remains an approved outcome.
