# Alpha 0.6D — Production 1L Serathi Captain

Production 1L carries the accepted Serathi Salt-Limestone Ledger language from Character Creator into the real Captain screen.

## Visual language

- Irregular warm limestone lintels, dark cedar uprights, and square hammered-copper joints remain the structural family.
- Terracotta cloth and masonry tones dominate the dossier and register leaves.
- Indigo and sea-green appear only as sparse colored-glass tesserae.
- Asterian starbursts, solar medallions, circular bosses, and continuous blue wave rails are excluded.
- Serathi culture remains distinct from Covenant faith; no religious emblem is used as generic cultural ornament.

## Runtime architecture

- The existing large portrait and identity dossier remain sticky beside the scrolling manuscript.
- History, Capabilities, and Condition remain independent live-height chapters.
- Each dossier, portrait mat, and chapter receives its own fitted nine-slice skin.
- Existing Serathi regional paintings occupy the profile vignette and chapter-header wells.
- The responsive fallback removes painted housings rather than stretching them across changed geometry.

## Scope

Character data, progression, relationships, standing, injury, navigation, combat, audio, and save behavior are unchanged. Skeldran and Asterian culture packs remain independent and untouched.
