# Alpha 0.6D R2 — Trade Law / Customs / Smuggling / Privateering / Wartime Law

## Ownership
R2 is a consumer/bridge layer, not a replacement simulation.

- A0.3C owns live political / war / emergency / religious / economic causes and policy tags.
- A0.1C owns market stock, prices and actual cargo transactions.
- A0.2A owns player knowledge of public information.
- A0.2D owns crime evidence, report transmission and authority receipt.
- R1 owns legal status, heat, standing and warrants.
- A0.1A owns terminal vessel state and prize resolution.

## Baseline trade law
Jurisdictions may define persistent customs rules by commodity legal class. Temporary embargoes, search orders, wartime restrictions and privateering authorization come from A0.3C policy tags.

## Customs
Arrival inspections derive from local customs capacity, controlled cargo, standing/heat, warrants and live search policy. The player may present papers / declare cargo or attempt concealment where applicable.

A successful concealment records crime truth without granting omniscient authority knowledge. A detected offense uses the existing local A0.2D direct-authority report path.

## Smuggling
Black-market access is based on settlement smuggling availability plus captain background/profession/Streetwise. Smuggled transactions still mutate the canonical A0.1C market and cargo state.

## Privateering and wartime law
Letters of marque require a live policy that opens privateering and specifies enemy factions. Authorized prize-taking still resolves through A0.1A. Without a valid commission, the same action remains subject to the existing piracy/crime path.

## Presentation
R2 uses concise legality/reason text in existing Market/port context, trade papers in Government, and customs choices on Arrival. It does not add a large customs or crime dashboard or redesign locked Character/Captain geometry.
